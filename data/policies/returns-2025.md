# Returns policy (2025)

This policy applied to purchases made from 1 January 2025 to
31 December 2025. It was replaced on 1 January 2026.

## How long you have

You can return an item within **30 days** of delivery.

## Condition

The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Cost of returning

You pay the return shipping. A return pickup costs 299 kr in Denmark.
Returns in a store are free.

## Refund

We refund the purchase price within 30 days of receiving the item.
