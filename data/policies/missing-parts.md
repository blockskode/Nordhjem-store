# Missing parts

If a screw, leg or other part is missing, order it through the contact
form. Give the product name and the part number from the assembly
instructions.

Missing parts are free, and sent within **5 working days**. Spare parts
for items older than the warranty can be bought for a small fee while
they are in stock.
