# Assembly service

Our assembly partner puts your furniture together in your home, on the
day of delivery or within 5 working days.

## Where

Assembly is available in **Denmark and Sweden only**. It is not
offered in Germany or Norway.

## Prices in Denmark

- Sofa or sofa bed: 499 kr
- Corner sofa: 799 kr
- Dining table: 399 kr
- Office chair: 199 kr

## Prices in Sweden

- Sofa or sofa bed: 599 SEK
- Corner sofa: 949 SEK
- Dining table: 499 SEK
- Office chair: 249 SEK

## Booking

Book assembly when you order, or up to 48 hours before delivery
through customer service.
