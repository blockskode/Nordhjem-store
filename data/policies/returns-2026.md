# Returns policy

This policy applies to everything bought from 1 January 2026. It
replaces the 2025 returns policy.

## How long you have

You can return an item within **60 days** of delivery, for any reason.
For online orders this is longer than the 14-day right of withdrawal
that EU law requires.

## Condition

The item must be unused and complete. It does not have to be in its
original packaging, but it must be packed so it is not damaged on the
way back.

## What cannot be returned

- Mattresses: see the 100-night mattress trial instead.
- Sofas made to order (your own choice of fabric and size).
- Items sold as "as-is" in the clearance corner of a store.
- Opened pillows and duvets, for hygiene reasons, unless faulty.

## Cost of returning

Returning an item in a store is free. A return pickup from your home
costs 349 kr in Denmark, 449 SEK in Sweden and 49 EUR in Germany. For
Norway, see the delivery policy for Norway.

## Refund

We refund the purchase price to the payment method you used, within
14 days of receiving the item. Delivery costs are not refunded.
