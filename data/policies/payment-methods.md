# Payment methods

## In all countries

Visa, Mastercard and Nordhjem gift cards.

## By country

- **Denmark**: MobilePay, Dankort.
- **Sweden**: Swish, Klarna.
- **Norway**: Vipps.
- **Germany**: Klarna, PayPal, bank transfer (SEPA).

## In stores

Stores take cards and the country's mobile payment. Cash is accepted
in Danish stores only.

## When you are charged

Online orders are charged when the order is shipped, not when you
place it.
