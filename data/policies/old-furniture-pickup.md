# Old furniture pickup

When we deliver a new sofa or mattress, we can take your old one with
us and bring it to recycling.

## Where and what

- In **Denmark only**.
- Sofas and mattresses only, one old item for each new item of the
  same kind.

## Price

299 kr per item. Book it together with your order. It cannot be booked
on its own, without a delivery.

Note: a mattress returned under the 100-night trial is always picked up
for free. That is a return, not an old-furniture pickup.
