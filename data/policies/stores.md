# Stores and opening hours

## Stores

- **Copenhagen** (Valby), Denmark
- **Aarhus**, Denmark
- **Malmö**, Sweden
- **Hamburg**, Germany

There are no Nordhjem stores in Norway.

## Opening hours

Monday to Friday 10 to 19, Saturday 10 to 17, Sunday 11 to 16. Stores
are closed on public holidays in their country.

## Parking

All stores have free parking.
