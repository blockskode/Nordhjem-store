# Price drop guarantee

## Our own prices

If the price of an item drops within **14 days** after you bought it,
we refund the difference. Send your order number through the contact
form.

## Not included

- Clearance and "as-is" items.
- Other shops' prices: we do not match competitors.
- Campaign codes that are only for members or newsletters.
