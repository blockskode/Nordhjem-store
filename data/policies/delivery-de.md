# Delivery in Germany

## Price and time

- Standard delivery: **59 EUR**, free on orders over 800 EUR.
- Delivery time: 4 to 8 working days.
- We deliver to the door on the ground floor.

## Delivery to the room

Two-person delivery to the room of your choice costs 99 EUR.

## Islands

We do not deliver to the North Sea and Baltic Sea islands.
