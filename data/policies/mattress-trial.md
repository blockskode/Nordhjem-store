# 100-night mattress trial

Every mattress from Nordhjem comes with a 100-night trial. If the
mattress is not right for you, we take it back.

## How it works

- The 100 nights start on the day the mattress is delivered.
- You must sleep on the mattress for at least **30 nights** before you
  can return it. Your body needs that long to get used to a new
  mattress.
- You can return it at any time between night 30 and night 100.

## Conditions

- The mattress must have been used with a mattress protector and be
  free of stains and damage.
- One trial per household per year.
- The trial applies to mattresses only, not to pillows, duvets or bed
  frames.

## Exchange or refund

You can exchange the mattress for another one, or get your money
back. We pick up the old mattress for free in all four countries.
The 100-night trial replaces the normal returns policy for
mattresses.
