# Sofa care

## Fabric

Vacuum weekly. Wipe spills straight away with a damp cloth, from the
outside in. Covers marked "removable" can be washed at 30 degrees.
Pet-friendly fabric resists claws and can be cleaned with water only.

## Leather

Dust with a dry cloth. Treat with leather care twice a year. Keep out
of direct sunlight to avoid fading and cracks. Never use soap,
alcohol or wet wipes.

## Velvet

Brush gently in the direction of the pile. Do not press wet stains;
let a professional cleaner handle them.

## Boucle

Vacuum with a soft brush. Do not pull loose loops; cut them off.

## Warranty

Damage from cleaning with products this guide warns against is not
covered by the warranty.
