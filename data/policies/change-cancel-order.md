# Changing or cancelling an order

## Cancelling

You can cancel an order free of charge until it has been shipped. After
that, the returns policy applies.

## Changing an order

You can change the delivery address or delivery day up to **48 hours**
before delivery. You cannot change the items in an order once it is
placed; cancel it and order again instead.

## Made-to-order sofas

A sofa made to order can only be cancelled within 24 hours of ordering,
because production starts straight away.
