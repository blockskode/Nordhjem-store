# Paying in instalments

## Denmark

Nordhjem Konto, through our finance partner: **0 % interest over 12
months** on orders over 3,000 kr. A one-off setup fee of 195 kr.

## Sweden and Germany

Pay later or in instalments with Klarna. Klarna's own terms apply.

## Norway

Instalments are not available in Norway.
