# Damaged or faulty items

## Damage during delivery

Check your delivery when it arrives. If an item is damaged, report it
within **14 days** of delivery through the contact form, with photos of
the damage and of the packaging. We then send a replacement part, a
new item, or give you a refund, at no cost to you.

## Faults that appear later

A fault that appears later is covered by the warranty. See the
warranty overview.

## Used items

An item that you have used and that is not faulty cannot be returned
as damaged. The normal returns policy applies.
