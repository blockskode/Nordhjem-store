# Delivery in Denmark

## Price and time

- Standard delivery: **499 kr**, free on orders over 5,000 kr.
- Delivery time: 3 to 7 working days.
- We deliver to the door on the ground floor, or to the kerb if there
  is no access.

## Delivery to the room

Two-person delivery to the room of your choice, on any floor, costs
899 kr. It is not free, whatever the order size.

## Islands

Delivery to Bornholm costs an extra 300 kr. Delivery to islands without
a bridge connection costs an extra 450 kr and takes up to 12 working
days.

## Tracking

You get a text message with a two-hour delivery window the day before.
