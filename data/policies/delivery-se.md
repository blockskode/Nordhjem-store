# Delivery in Sweden

## Price and time

- Standard delivery: **599 SEK**, free on orders over 7,000 SEK.
- Delivery time: 5 to 9 working days.
- We deliver to the door on the ground floor.

## Delivery to the room

Two-person delivery to the room of your choice costs 999 SEK.

## Northern Sweden

Deliveries north of Sundsvall take up to 14 working days. The price is
the same.
