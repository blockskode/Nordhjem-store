# Privacy

## What we keep

Your name, address, email and phone number, to deliver orders and
answer questions. Your order history, for returns and warranty.

## How long

Order data is kept for 5 years, as accounting law requires. Questions
asked in the chat are not linked to your name and are deleted after
90 days.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data through the contact
form.
