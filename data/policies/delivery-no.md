# Delivery in Norway

Norway is not part of the EU, so some rules are different from our
other countries.

## Price and time

- Standard delivery: **799 NOK**. There is no free delivery in Norway,
  whatever the order size.
- Delivery time: 7 to 12 working days, because the goods pass customs.

## Customs and VAT

Prices shown on the Norwegian shop include Norwegian VAT (25 %). We
handle customs, so you pay nothing extra on delivery.

## Returns from Norway

You have the same 60 days to return an item. A return pickup in
Norway costs **599 NOK**. There are no Nordhjem stores in Norway, so
returns cannot be made in a store.

## Not available in Norway

Assembly and old-furniture pickup are not offered in Norway.
