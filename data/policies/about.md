# About Nordhjem

Nordhjem is a furniture shop founded in Aarhus in 2014. We sell sofas,
mattresses, rugs, dining tables, office chairs, garden furniture,
pillows and duvets.

## Where we sell

Online in **Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Germany**, and in four stores.
We do not deliver to other countries.

## Our promise

Calm, durable furniture at fair prices, with a 60-day returns policy
and a 100-night mattress trial.

Note: Nordhjem is a fictional shop made for a learning project. All
names, prices and rules are invented.
