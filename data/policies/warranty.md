# Warranty overview

## Your legal right

In Denmark, Sweden and Germany, you can complain about a fault for
**2 years** after delivery. In Norway the law gives **5 years** for
items that are meant to last much longer than 2 years, such as sofas,
tables and mattresses.

## Nordhjem's extra warranty

On top of the law, we give:

- **Office chairs**: 5 years on the frame and the gas lift.
- **Dining tables**: 5 years on the frame.
- **Garden furniture**: 3 years on aluminium and teak frames.
- **Mattresses**: see the mattress warranty.

## What is not covered

Normal wear, damage from misuse, stains, fading from sunlight, and
cleaning with products that the care guide warns against.

## How to claim

Use the contact form with your order number and photos. We repair,
replace, or refund.
