# Mattress warranty

## What is covered

- Springs and foam core: **10 years**.
- A dip of more than 2.5 cm without anyone lying on the mattress.

## What is not covered

- Softening of the comfort layer of less than 2.5 cm. This is normal.
- Stains, burns and damage from not using a mattress protector.
- The cover: 2 years, like other textiles.

## Not the same as the trial

The warranty is for faults. If the mattress is simply not comfortable
for you, see the 100-night trial.
