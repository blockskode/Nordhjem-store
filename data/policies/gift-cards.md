# Gift cards

## Validity

A gift card is valid for **3 years** from the day it was bought.

## Where it can be used

Online and in stores, but only in the country where it was bought. A
Danish gift card cannot be used in Sweden.

## Rules

A gift card cannot be exchanged for cash. If you return an item paid
for with a gift card, you get a new gift card.
